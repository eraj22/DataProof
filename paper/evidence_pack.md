# Phase 2 evidence pack

Use this as a source ledger while writing the paper in your own words. Do not copy it as the submitted paper.

## Dataset and split

- Source: processed workbooks distributed with DSBench.
- Derived benchmark: 63 contract-compatible tasks from seven detected workbook tables.
- Development: 9 tasks; validation: 18 tasks; final reserved split: 36 tasks.
- Main completed pre-improvement evaluation: 54 tasks across the original fixed evaluation split, three repeats (162 runs). These questions are derived from DSBench workbooks and are not the official DSBench questions.
- The post-improvement 36-task rerun was interrupted by prolonged network failures and must not be reported as complete.

## Claims the evidence supports

- The complete system can execute the constrained aggregate, filtered aggregate and ambiguity tasks end to end with logged trajectories.
- The verifier detects the controlled value corruptions used in E4 while accepting the clean outputs in that experiment.
- On the nine-task pilot, removing the abstention controller reduced appropriate outcomes on ambiguous requests.

## Claims the evidence does not support

- Reproducing the official DSBench score or outperforming DSBench on its official questions.
- General data-science automation beyond the allowlisted operations.
- A general verifier benefit from natural model failures; the no-verifier pilot tied the full system.
- Statistical generalization beyond the selected workbooks and derived question templates.
